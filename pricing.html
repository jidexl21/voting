Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing.

How does pricing work?

The Free plan is free forever. Starter and Growth are billed once per election you run — you only pay when you launch a vote, not every month. Enterprise is custom-quoted.

What's included in the Free plan?

Up to 100 voters and one election per year, with real-time results and audit trail. ELECTS ONLINE branding is shown on your ballots.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments are processed securely by Monnify and Paystack: debit/credit card, bank transfer, USSD, and mobile money. Prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦).

Can I change plans later?

Yes — choose a higher plan when you create your next election. Voter and concurrent-election limits apply per the plan you select at checkout.

Ready to run your first election?

Your first election is free. Create an account and set up a secure, transparent vote in minutes.
